## 摘要
带有铰接旋翼的系留自转旋翼机可以作为一种无人机运行，通过利用环境风能维持飞行，实现节能、长时间的部署。本文针对此类系统提出了一种基于模型的态度控制技术，并使用全保真动态模型。结合拉格朗日方法、叶素动量理论和悬链线力学，将先前开发的二维混合模型扩展到三维。新模型描述了框架的完整刚体运动，包括滚转和偏航动力学，并为每个叶片增加了旋翼速度和挥舞自由度。通过稳态响应检查平衡特性，并与先前模型进行比较。发现平衡趋势与文献中报告的一致。开发了一种基于再生差动旋翼制动的反馈控制策略，以调节所有三个姿态角。仿真证明了有效的姿态调节和稳定飞行。

## Abstract
A tethered autogyro with articulated rotors can operate as an unmanned aerial vehicle capable of energy-efficient, long-duration deployment by utilizing ambient wind energy to sustain flight. This article presents a model-based attitude control technique for such a system using a full-fidelity dynamic model. Using Lagrangian approach combined with Blade Element Momentum Theory and catenary mechanics, a previously developed 2D hybrid model is extended to three dimensions. The new model describes the complete rigid-body motion of the frame, including roll and yaw dynamics, and is augmented with rotor speed and flapping degrees of freedom for each blade. Equilibrium characteristics are examined through steady-state responses and compared with the prior model. The resulting trends of equilibria are found to be consistent with those reported in the literature. A feedback control strategy based on regenerative differential rotor braking is developed to modulate all three attitude angles. Simulations demonstrate effective attitude regulation and stable flight.
