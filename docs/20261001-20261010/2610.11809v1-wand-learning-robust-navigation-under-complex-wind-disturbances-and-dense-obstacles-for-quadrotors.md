## 摘要
在杂乱环境中实现鲁棒导航仍然是四旋翼飞行器的一项基本挑战，尤其是当出现强风扰时，风扰会扰动飞行器动力学、限制控制权限，并显著增加碰撞风险。现有的基于学习的导航策略通常依赖障碍物感知和本体感知观测，要求策略隐式推断时变扰动影响，从而限制了部分可观测条件下的鲁棒性。本文提出 WAND（Wind-Aware Navigation with Disturbance Estimation，带扰动估计的风感知导航），一种用于密集障碍场中时变风扰下导航的强化学习框架。具体而言，WAND 使用时序卷积网络（TCN）从历史本体感知状态中估计风致扰动加速度。该估计通过零初始化残差模块 WindAdapter 集成到策略中，同时为底层控制提供前馈补偿。对该估计的双重利用将扰动条件导航与前馈扰动抑制耦合起来。在 12 种风扰仿真设置中，WAND 相较于仅使用前馈补偿，平均将观测成功率提高了 8.3 个百分点。受控的相反侧风实验进一步显示了依赖风向的轨迹自适应。在室内风扇诱导飞行测试中，WAND 在 20 次试验中成功了 18 次，证明了实时机载导航的可行性。

## Abstract
Robust navigation in cluttered environments remains a fundamental challenge for quadrotors, particularly when strong wind disturbances arise, which perturb vehicle dynamics, limit control authority, and substantially increase collision risk. Existing learning-based navigation policies typically rely on obstacle perception and proprioceptive observations, requiring the policy to infer time-varying disturbance effects implicitly and thereby limiting robustness under partial observability. This paper proposes WAND (Wind-Aware Navigation with Disturbance Estimation), a reinforcement learning framework for navigation under time-varying wind disturbances in dense obstacle fields. Specifically, WAND estimates wind-induced disturbance acceleration from historical proprioceptive states using a Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN). This estimation is integrated into the policy via a zero-initialized residual module, \emph{WindAdapter}, while simultaneously providing feedforward compensation for low-level control. The dual use of the estimate couples disturbance-conditioned navigation with feedforward disturbance rejection. Across 12 wind-disturbed simulation settings, WAND improved the observed success rate by 8.3 percentage points on average relative to feedforward compensation alone. Controlled opposite-crosswind experiments further showed wind-direction-dependent trajectory adaptation. In indoor fan-induced flight tests, WAND succeeded in 18 of 20 trials, demonstrating the feasibility of real-time onboard navigation.
