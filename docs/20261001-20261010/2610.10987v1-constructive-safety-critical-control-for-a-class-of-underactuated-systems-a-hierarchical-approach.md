## 摘要
具有非平凡几何结构的欠驱动系统在实际机器人问题中广泛存在。然而，在存在欠驱动的情况下，关于构造性安全关键控制设计，人们所知甚少。在这项工作中，我们为一类四旋翼式欠驱动系统提供了一个构造性框架，用于综合安全控制架构和控制障碍函数（CBF）。通过研究其欠驱动的几何结构，我们刻画了这些系统何时具有对安全关键控制设计有用的自然分层架构。利用这些架构，我们设计了一种构造性的分层 CBF 综合流程，并给出了其成功的条件。

## Abstract
Underactuated systems with nontrivial geometry are abundant in practical robotic problems. However, little is known about constructive safety-critical control design in the presence of underactuation. In this work, we provide a constructive framework for synthesizing safe control architectures and control barrier functions for a class of quadrotor-like underactuated systems. By studying the geometry of their underactuation, we characterize when these systems have natural layered architectures useful for safety-critical control design. Using these architectures, we devise a constructive, hierarchical CBF synthesis procedure and provide conditions under which it succeeds.
