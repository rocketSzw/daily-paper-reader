## 摘要
我们提出了一种针对受状态约束的固定翼飞机的滚动时域最优控制。这些约束包括运行约束，如高度、地理围栏和障碍物规避，以及航迹角、滚转角和空速的边界。状态约束被组合成一个单一的软最小值控制障碍函数（CBF）。我们在约束近似动态规划中使用该复合CBF，以获得一系列解析闭式控制函数，这些函数在CBF约束下近似最小化二次有限时域积分代价。所得的滚动时域控制是非短视的，因为它在整个预测时域内的所有时刻满足状态约束的同时近似优化积分代价。我们在固定翼飞机在风不确定性下穿越充满障碍物的空域的仿真中，展示了约束满足情况和性能。我们还将该滚动时域控制与另外两种方法进行了比较。

## Abstract
We present a receding-horizon optimal control for fixed-wing aircraft subject to state constraints. These constraints include operational constraints such as altitude, geofencing, and obstacle avoidance, as well as bounds on flight-path angle, roll angle, and airspeed. The state constraints are composed into a single soft-minimum control barrier function (CBF). We use this composite CBF in a constrained-approximate dynamic program to obtain a sequence of analytic closed-form control functions that approximately minimize a quadratic finite-horizon integral cost subject to the CBF constraint. The resulting receding-horizon control is non-myopic in the sense that it approximately optimizes the integral cost while satisfying the state constraint at all times along the entire prediction horizon. We demonstrate constraint satisfaction and performance in simulations of a fixed-wing aircraft navigating an obstacle-filled airspace under wind uncertainty. We also compare this receding-horizon control with 2 other methods.
