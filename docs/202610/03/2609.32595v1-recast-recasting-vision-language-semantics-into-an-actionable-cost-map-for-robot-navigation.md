## 摘要
在多样环境中实现安全且鲁棒的机器人导航，需要对复杂场景的高层理解，并具备将这种理解转化为稳定运动的能力。近期工作通过大规模训练的基于学习的模型，以及基于视觉-语言模型（VLM）的方法来解决这一问题。然而，基于学习的模型在训练分布之外会失效，而基于 VLM 的方法虽带来了这种理解，却很少将其落地到场景中，也未能使动作与之对齐。为解决这些局限，我们提出 RECAST，一个机器人导航框架，将 VLM 的推理与视觉基础模型的空间定位相结合，以构建可执行代价地图。给定机器人的前视视角和用户指令，我们首先利用 VLM 分解场景，判断哪些表面可通行、哪些物体构成风险、应偏好哪个朝向，以及哪些间隙可通过。随后，视觉基础模型在图像中定位这些表面和物体，并将这四项判断在空间上重铸为一张紧凑的代价地图。该地图既为轨迹解码器提供条件，又对其候选轨迹进行评分，以选择要执行的一条。由于 VLM 的回答滞后于实时场景，这两个步骤都利用来自两个时间点的代价地图：VLM 所判断的枢轴帧（承载全部四项判断），以及当前帧（其地形和碰撞代价由当前图像重建）。RECAST 在仿真中将成功率较最强先前方法提升 13.3 个百分点，在真实四足机器人上提升 31.4 个百分点，并将碰撞率相对其降低 9.6 和 14.3 个百分点，达到所有方法中最低的碰撞率。项目页面见 https://recast-nav.github.io/

## Abstract
Safe and robust robot navigation across diverse environments requires a high-level understanding of complex scenes and the ability to carry it into stable motion. Recent works tackle this with learning-based models trained at scale and with approaches built on vision-language models (VLMs). However, learning-based models break down outside their training distribution, while VLM-based approaches bring that understanding but rarely ground it in the scene or align the action with it. To address these limitations, we present RECAST, a robot navigation framework that combines the reasoning of a VLM with the spatial grounding of vision foundation models to build an Actionable Cost map. Given the robot's front view and the user's instruction, we first decompose the scene with the VLM, judging which surfaces are traversable, which objects pose a risk, which heading to prefer, and which gaps are passable. Vision foundation models then ground these surfaces and objects in the image, and all four judgments are spatially recast into one compact cost map. This map both conditions the trajectory decoders and scores their proposals to select the one to execute. As the VLM's answers trail the live scene, both steps draw on cost maps from two points in time: the pivot frame the VLM judged, which carries all four judgments, and the current frame, whose terrain and collision costs are rebuilt from the current image. RECAST improves success over the strongest prior method by 13.3 points in simulation and 31.4 points on a real quadruped, and reduces the collision rate relative to it by 9.6 and 14.3 points, reaching the lowest collision rate among all methods. The project page is available at https://recast-nav.github.io/
