## 摘要
本文报道了三种用于全驱动欧拉-拉格朗日系统的自适应全局跟踪控制器，与现有设计相比具有可验证的性能提升。其中两种控制器在弱区间激励条件下可确保全局指数收敛。此外，所提出的一种控制器具有简单的类自适应PID结构，与经典方案不同，它无需额外滤波。我们采用复合自适应架构，引入一种新的系统动力学参数化方法，并使用近期文献中提出的一种高性能估计方案。在二自由度机械臂上进行的实时实验以及与一种基于学习的自适应控制器的对比研究，验证了所提出控制器的有效性。

## Abstract
Three adaptive global tracking controllers for fully actuated Euler-Lagrange systems, with verifiable performance improvement over existing designs, are reported in this letter. Two of these controllers ensure global exponential convergence under a weak interval excitation condition. Besides, one of the proposed controllers features a simple adaptive PID-like structure that-unlike classical solutions-avoids the need for additional filtering. We adopt a composite adaptation architecture, invoke a novel parameterization of the system dynamics and use a high performance estimation scheme recently introduced in the literature. Real-time experiments and a comparative study with a learning-based adaptive controller on a two-degrees-of-freedom manipulator arm illustrate the effectiveness of the proposed controllers.
